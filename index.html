Fly from Ohio State to orbit with ARCHIVE

A 2½-minute skyship flight through time: The Ohio State University's campus as it is today, built from real map data; downtown Columbus in one imagined 2050; then up through the clouds to an orbital habitat where food, water, energy and air are made and recycled.

Watch the flight Explore at your pace Learn with questions

Campus and city streets and buildings: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Columbus 2050 is an artistic future scenario, not an official plan. Works in desktop and phone browsers and in WebXR headsets.
